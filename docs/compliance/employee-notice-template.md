[Company] uses UseJunction to understand how AI coding tools (for example Cursor, Claude, Codex) are used: request volume, estimated cost, plan and seat utilization, and whether devices are covered.

**Your rights.** You can open **My data** in UseJunction to see what was uploaded, export a copy, and request erasure. You can also contact [privacy contact / HR].
